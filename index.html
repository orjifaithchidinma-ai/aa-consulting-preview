FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What solutions does AA-Consult provide?

AA-Consult delivers end-to-end enterprise security architectures tailored to modern threat landscapes. We specialize in Privileged Access Management (PAM), Identity & Access Management (IAM), Next-Gen Perimeter Firewalls, patchless vulnerability remediation, automated XDR platforms, and comprehensive Cyber GRC systems. Through our strategic partnerships with industry leaders like Palo Alto Networks, CyberArk, Vicarius, Cynet, and Commugen, we ensure your organization is equipped with cohesive, best-in-class defense mechanisms that seamlessly integrate across your entire digital infrastructure.

Which geographic regions do you serve?

While our global headquarters is strategically positioned in Israel to leverage cutting-edge cyber innovation and R&D insights, we maintain dedicated regional deployment and engineering offices in Lagos, Nigeria, and Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. From these operational hubs, our field teams provide active, hands-on enterprise support, compliance alignment, and rapid-response capabilities for clients spanning across West, East, and the broader Sub-Saharan African digital markets.

Do you provide local technical implementation and ongoing support?

Yes, absolutely. We go far beyond simple license provisioning. Our team consists of certified security engineers and deployment specialists who manage the complete lifecycle of your security stack. This includes initial architecture design, complex integrations, custom configuration, thorough employee training workshops, and round-the-clock (24/7) technical escalation support handled locally to guarantee minimal downtime and immediate threat mitigation.

How can we schedule an infrastructure audit or product demo?

Initiating an audit or arranging a live, personalized platform walkthrough is simple. You can fill out our brief contact form at the bottom of the index page or click the "Book a Demo" / "Book a Consultation" buttons accessible across any section of our site. Once submitted, a dedicated solutions engineer will review your requirements and reach out within 24 hours to organize a consultation specifically mapped to your organization's technology stack and risk profile.
