Data Governance

Maintaining control and visibility over sensitive data across on-prem, cloud, and APIs.

The Challenge

IT infrastructure expands daily as organizations move to the cloud, creating a massive challenge to maintain control over sensitive data. Service accounts and unauthorized users frequently operate with broad permissions without real-time enforcement, leaving information exposed and creating significant compliance gaps.

The Solution

SecuPi evaluates every request in real-time to verify identity and context. By using Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC), dynamic masking, and row-level filtering, it ensures users see only what they are authorized to see, enforcing Zero Trust at the data source.

The Challenge

With over 96% of data breaches involving the database layer, organizations often have "blind spots" where rogue insiders or compromised accounts exfiltrate massive amounts of sensitive data without ever triggering traditional network alarms or firewall rules.

The Solution

We deploy Trellix DAM to provide continuous monitoring and automated alerts for all database transactions. By configuring custom rules for failed login attempts and sensitive table access, we ensure full compliance with PCI-DSS and GDPR in real-time.

Database Security

Real-time auditing and protection for banking and enterprise databases.

Identity Security

Securing the highest level of access within your organization through industry-leading vaulting.

The Challenge

Privileged credentials are the top target for hackers. Once compromised, they allow total control over your IT environment. Without a central vault, passwords are often stored in plain text or never rotated, making it easy for threats to maintain long-term persistence.

The Solution

We implement CyberArk to isolate, rotate, and record privileged sessions. By using Just-In-Time (JIT) access and secure vaulting technology, we ensure that every administrative action is verified, logged, and secure against credential theft.

The Challenge

Security teams are drowned in thousands of alerts every day. Without business context and risk prioritization, it is impossible to know which vulnerability poses a real, immediate threat, often leading to "vulnerability fatigue" and critical flaws being ignored.

The Solution

Rapid7 InsightVM provides unified visibility across your entire infrastructure. We prioritize risks based on actual exploitability using Real Risk Scores, allowing your team to focus on the 1% of vulnerabilities that truly matter to your business impact.

GRC & Automation

Streamlining governance, risk, and compliance through intelligent automation.

The Challenge

Enterprises often struggle with fragmented compliance data and manual risk assessments. Keeping up with evolving regulatory frameworks like ISO or local financial mandates becomes an administrative burden prone to human error and missed deadlines.

The Solution

We implement Commugen’s GRC 365 platform to automate and unify your compliance posture. By centralizing risk management into a single dashboard, we enable real-time visibility and ensure your organization remains audit-ready without manual overhead.

The Challenge

Sophisticated payment fraud and internal misconduct cost institutions millions annually. Traditional monitoring often flags too many false positives or fails to detect subtle, behavioral patterns that indicate a compromised account or a rogue employee.

The Solution

Bottomline CFRM provides a behavior-based layer of defense. We deploy this to monitor every transaction, using advanced analytics to stop fraudulent payments in their tracks and provide a forensic audit trail of all sensitive financial activity.

Patchless Protection

Remediating software risks without the downtime of traditional patching.

The Challenge

Traditional patching often breaks applications and requires downtime. Furthermore, many legacy systems remain unpatched because vendors no longer support them, leaving them permanently exposed to known exploits that cannot be fixed through regular updates.

The Solution

Vicarius VRX standout feature, Patchless Protection, allows us to secure applications at the binary level. This shields your infrastructure from exploits even when a vendor patch hasn't been released or if the system is too critical to take offline.
