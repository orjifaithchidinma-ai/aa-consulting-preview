Data Governance

Maintaining control and visibility over sensitive data across on-prem, cloud, and APIs.

The Challenge

IT infrastructure expands daily as organizations move to the cloud and adopt new APIs, creating a massive challenge to maintain control over sensitive data. Service accounts, applications, and unauthorized users frequently operate with broad permissions without real-time enforcement. This leaves sensitive information exposed, increasing insider risk and creating significant compliance gaps for data privacy regulations.

The Solution

SecuPi addresses this by enforcing governance and security directly where data is stored and accessed. It evaluates every single request in real-time to verify identity, context, and policy compliance. By using **Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC)**, dynamic masking, and row-level filtering, SecuPi ensures users see only what they are authorized to see, enforcing Zero Trust at the data source.

The Challenge

Perimeter security is no longer enough to protect modern enterprises. With over 96% of data breaches involving the database layer, organizations often have "blind spots" where rogue insiders or compromised service accounts exfiltrate massive amounts of sensitive data without ever triggering traditional network alarms or firewall rules.

The Solution

We deploy Trellix DAM to provide continuous monitoring and automated alerts for all database transactions. By configuring custom rules for failed login attempts and sensitive table access, we ensure full compliance with **PCI-DSS** and **GDPR**, while providing the capability to block suspicious queries in real-time before data is compromised.

Identity Security (PAM)

Securing the highest level of access within your organization through industry-leading vaulting.

The Challenge

Privileged credentials are the top target for hackers. Once compromised, they allow an attacker total control over your IT environment, enabling lateral movement and silent data exfiltration. Without a central vault, passwords are often stored in plain text or never rotated, making it easy for threats to maintain long-term persistence in your system.

The Solution

We implement CyberArk to isolate, rotate, and record privileged sessions. By using **Just-In-Time (JIT)** access and secure vaulting technology, we ensure that every administrative action is verified, logged, and secure. This eliminates the risk of credential theft and ensures that only the right people have the right access at the right time.

The Challenge

Security teams are drowned in thousands of alerts every day. Without business context and risk prioritization, it is impossible to know which vulnerability poses a real, immediate threat to the business infrastructure. This "vulnerability fatigue" often leads to critical flaws being ignored while teams spend time on low-risk issues.

The Solution

Rapid7 InsightVM provides unified visibility across your entire infrastructure. We help prioritize risks based on actual exploitability and attacker behavior using **Real Risk Scores**. This allows your security team to stop "finding bugs" and start "reducing impact" by focusing on the 1% of vulnerabilities that truly matter.

Patchless Protection

Remediating software risks without the downtime of traditional patching.

The Challenge

Traditional patching often breaks applications and requires significant downtime, leading to friction between security and IT teams. Furthermore, many legacy systems remain unpatched because vendors no longer support them, leaving them permanently exposed to known exploits that cannot be fixed through regular updates.

The Solution

Vicarius VRX provides a single, cohesive platform for finding and fixing vulnerabilities. Its standout feature, **Patchless Protection**, allows us to secure applications at the binary level. This shields your infrastructure from exploits even when a vendor patch hasn't been released or if the system is too critical to take offline.
