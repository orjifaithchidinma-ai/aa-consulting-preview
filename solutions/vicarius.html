THE VULNERABILITY MANAGEMENT CHALLENGE

Too many vulnerabilities. Not enough time.

Security teams are constantly flooded with thousands of vulnerability alerts, making it difficult to distinguish between theoretical risks and active exploits threatening the environment.

Traditional vulnerability assessment tools often generate static reports without actionable remediation paths, leaving IT and security silos struggling to patch systems before attackers strike.

The Core Goal

Streamline vulnerability discovery, contextual prioritization, automated patching, and innovative patchless protection across all enterprise assets.

OUR VICARIUS SOLUTION

Autonomous Vulnerability & Patchless Protection

In partnership with Vicarius, AA-Consulting empowers organizations with an end-to-end vulnerability remediation and patchless protection platform designed to secure systems even when official patches are unavailable or pending deployment.

The platform equips security operations with continuous asset discovery, rich context mapping, and built-in defense mechanisms to shrink the window of exposure.
