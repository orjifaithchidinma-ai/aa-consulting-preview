THE IDENTITY SECURITY CHALLENGE

Managing High–Risk Access Across the Modern Enterprise

Privileged accounts and credentials provide powerful access to an organization's most sensitive resources. If these identities are compromised, attackers can gain unauthorized entry, move laterally across systems, and compromise core assets.

Organizations must balance security with agility—granting legitimate users, admins, third parties, and applications access without exposing the environment to unaccountable risks.

The Core Goal

Ensure access is strictly controlled, limited, monitored, and fully accountable.

OUR CYBERARK SOLUTION

Proactive PAM Implementation

In partnership with CyberArk, AA-Consulting implements Privileged Access Management (PAM) capabilities designed to secure credentials and protect high-risk access paths.

By applying principles like Least Privilege and Just-In-Time access, CyberArk shields sensitive accounts across on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments from exploitation.

CyberArk PAM Core Capabilities
